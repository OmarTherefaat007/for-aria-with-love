Dear Aria,

Happy birthday to one of the most important people in my life. Almost five years ago, I had no idea that our friendship would become one of the places I feel safest, most understood, and most grateful for.

You have been there through so much. In the hard moments, you helped me get through things that felt heavier than I knew how to carry. Sometimes you did it with a conversation, sometimes with a laugh, and sometimes simply by being there. You may not always realize what that means to me, but I do. I remember it, and I carry that gratitude with me.

You have a way of making an ordinary day feel lighter. The world feels a little less difficult when I know you're in it. I hope you know how much your kindness, your humor, your honesty, and the way you show up for the people you love matter. You are so easy to appreciate, and so impossible to replace.

I'm grateful for every chapter we've shared so far: for the laughter, the quiet moments, the honest talks, and all the little things that became ours without us even noticing. I don't need to make up grand memories to know this friendship is special. The truth is already more than enough: having you as my best friend has changed my life for the better.

On your birthday, I hope life gives some of that goodness back to you. I hope this next year brings you peaceful mornings, big laughs, new things to look forward to, and people who remind you that you deserve to be cared for. I hope you feel proud of the person you are and excited for all the things still waiting for you.

Thank you for being my person through so much, and for making these almost five years mean more than I can fit into a letter. No matter where life takes us, I'll always be rooting for you. You deserve every good thing coming your way, Aria.

Happy birthday. I'm so lucky to call you my best friend.

Always in your corner,

Omar
